# Freight Capacity Auction Clearing Engine — Product Brief

## Users
- VP Logistics, freight procurement lead, freight broker operations leader
- Tenant admins, auction managers, procurement analysts, carrier viewers

## Positioning
Defensible freight spot-auction clearing that prices service risk instead of blindly choosing the cheapest bid.

## Product Personality
Dense, precise, operations-grade, explainable, privacy-aware, and audit-first. The UI should feel like a trusted freight operations console rather than generic SaaS.

## Trust Requirements
- Every award and rejection explains binding constraints.
- Sealed-bid privacy is visible in carrier views.
- Frozen report snapshots prove historical exports did not drift.
- Optional integrations are clearly disabled/degraded without blocking core work.

## Anti-References
No generic SaaS gradients, unlabeled red/green risk-only colors, opaque solver "AI magic", or hidden service-risk tradeoffs.
